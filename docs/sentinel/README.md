# Sentinel CAD

Onshape is the CAD source of truth:
[Sentinel_Drone assembly](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/31c7cfca8b01a8e37454827d/w/367f46a9ca4ab2a8ba035e51/e/1600b2c66d3b102227c2adfb).
Numeric geometry, masses and verification microversions live in
`models/sentinel-quad/information.json`. The assembly frame has +X toward the rear,
+Z up and millimetre units, and the rotor centre is at X 98.5, Y −59.

## Document folders

- **Sentinel Drone**: the `Sentinel_Drone` top-level assembly, plus
  - *Airframe & electronics (official imports)*: the imported Sentinel subassemblies,
    the compact parts studio and the salvaged F450 landing legs.
  - *Propellers*: the representative 1045 propeller design and pair.
- **Payload holders**
  - *Modular under-body carrier (current)*: the parametric Feature Studio and print parts.
  - *Archive (superseded holders)*: the rear cantilever platform, modular sliding tray,
    integrated side-slide tray, deep hanging cradle (v1) and dovetail carrier (v2). These are kept for history
    and are suppressed or absent in `Sentinel_Drone`.
- **SO-101 One-Arm**: the arm parts, follower assembly, STEP source and wrist-camera mount.
- **Scratch (empty tabs)**: empty tabs that can be deleted.

## Under-body carrier

The carrier is three separate printed PETG parts, generated by the `boltedCarrier`
feature. Nothing is printed onto the tray, and every joint is screwed:

- Two identical **side bars**. The assembly uses the second copy rotated 180° about the
  rotor axis. Each bar's top tab screws to the drone's bottom plate through the existing
  2.8 mm holes at X 85/112: one bar uses the Y −90 row and the other the Y −28 row.
  These are M2.5 screws from above into captive nuts in the tab. Each bar is a 120 mm
  tall web with gussets at both tabs.
- A flat, rimmed **tray**, 140 × 100 mm, for the arm to place objects on. The bars' bottom
  tabs sit on the tray and bolt through it with four M3 screws and nuts. Its top is 120 mm below the bottom
  plate, which leaves 20 mm of clearance above the landing-leg feet. It has a 20 mm grid
  of 3.2 mm fixture holes.

The feature parameters are tray length, width, drop and rim height. Two settings were
clash-checked against the drone and the landing legs: the default 140 × 100 mm with a
120 mm drop, and 160 × 120 mm with a 130 mm drop. In both, the bars clear the leg
flanges by 2.37 mm. Earlier holder iterations, including the dovetail carrier (v2),
are archived.

## Landing gear

The RB5 landing gear is suppressed. It is replaced by four salvaged YoungRC F450 legs,
modelled from a public F450 leg CAD (not official YoungRC geometry). The legs are fixed
under the bottom plate, splayed diagonally, with the feet on z −144.8. No plate-hole
set matched the leg flange pattern, so the leg positions are assumptions that keep the
legs clear of the RC receiver, the ESC stack and the carrier. Replace them with the
measured mounting holes when available.

## Limitations

Fastener lengths, side-bar stiffness under sideways loads, payload rating, print orientation, leg
material and mass, and the physical leg positions are unverified.
