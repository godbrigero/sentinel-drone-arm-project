# Sentinel CAD

Onshape is the CAD source of truth:
[Sentinel_Drone assembly](https://cad.onshape.com/documents/31c7cfca8b01a8e37454827d/w/367f46a9ca4ab2a8ba035e51/e/1600b2c66d3b102227c2adfb).
Numeric geometry, masses and verification microversions live in
`models/sentinel-quad/information.json`. The assembly frame has +X toward the rear,
+Z up and millimetre units, and the rotor centre is at X 98.5, Y −59.

## Document folders

- **Sentinel Drone**: the `Sentinel_Drone` top-level assembly, plus
  - *Airframe & electronics (official imports)*: the imported Sentinel subassemblies,
    the compact parts studio and the salvaged F450 landing legs.
  - *Propellers*: the representative 1045 propeller design and pair.
- **Payload holders**
  - *Modular under-body carrier (current)*: the parametric Feature Studio, the print parts
    and the 256 mm print-plate assembly.
  - *Archive (superseded holders)*: the rear cantilever platform, modular sliding tray,
    integrated side-slide tray, deep hanging cradle (v1), dovetail carrier (v2), bolted
    side-bar carrier (v3), hub-and-strut carrier (v4) and two-arm rail carrier (v5). These are kept for history
    and are suppressed or absent in `Sentinel_Drone`.
- **SO-101 One-Arm**: the arm parts, follower assembly, STEP source and wrist-camera mount.
- **Scratch (empty tabs)**: empty tabs that can be deleted.

## Under-body carrier

The carrier is two identical arms and a tray, printed in PETG and generated by the
`legMountedCarrier` feature, about 69 g in total (CAD). All joints use M2.5 screws.

- **Arms** (print 2). The assembly uses the second copy rotated 180° about X 98.5,
  Y −59. Each arm hangs from the two leg flanges on its side. Under each flange a 3 mm
  U-shaped pad sits on the flat underside, its tines either side of the leg neck and
  centre rib, bridged beyond the flange's inboard end. A chord joins the two pads inboard
  of the RB5 gear-mount bracket. Each pad takes both of the flange's existing screws:
  replace them with screws 3 mm longer, up through pad, flange and bottom plate into
  the arm standoffs. A 3 mm lattice web, filleted into the plate, runs down to the tray
  edge. The ledge at its foot carries the tray.
- **Tray**: 140 × 112 mm, with a 1.2 mm floor and 1.2 mm × 4 mm rims. It is thickened
  only at four bosses where M2.5 screws come up through the arm ledges into captive
  nuts. Its top is 120 mm below the bottom plate.

Edges are rounded at 0.5 mm, or 0.3 mm where the walls are too thin, and are otherwise
left sharp. The arms clear the legs, the ESC board and its nylon spacers (closest 1.7 mm)
and the RB5 mount brackets. Earlier iterations are archived.

### Printing

The *Print plate (256 mm bed)* assembly lays out both arms and the tray for one print
on a 256 × 256 mm bed, 10 mm apart, within X 8.6–247.4 and Y 47.5–208.5 mm. Export it
as 3MF from Onshape to slice it. Print the arms upside down, with the pad faces
(1392 mm² each) on the bed; they stand 120.3 mm tall. Only the 9 mm tray ledge at the top
overhangs (281 mm² each), so support that and nothing else. A brim helps the tall, narrow
arms stay down. Print the tray floor down without supports.

## Landing gear

The RB5 landing gear is suppressed. It is replaced by four salvaged YoungRC F450 legs,
modelled from a public F450 leg CAD (not official YoungRC geometry). As on the hardware
(checked against an underside photo), one leg sits under each arm root, splayed along
the arm diagonal. Its flange is flush under the bottom plate and its outer hole pair takes
the two arm-standoff screws; the inner pair is empty. The feet are on z −144.8. The CAD
flange's outer holes are 21.0 mm apart and the plate holes 19.8 mm, so the modelled holes
sit about 0.6 mm off the screw axes.

On the hardware the black RB5 gear-mount brackets stay screwed to the middle of each
side edge; in CAD they are suppressed with the rest of the RB5 gear. The official
Spektrum receiver part overlaps the front-left flange and pad; the photo shows no
receiver there, so its CAD position does not match this aircraft.

## Simulation model

`models/sentinel-quad/sentinel.xml` is generated from the `Sentinel_Drone` assembly
by `scripts/sentinel/build_model.py`. Onshape remains the source: to rebuild after a
CAD change, export the assembly as glTF (resolution COARSE, hidden parts excluded,
Z up), update `simulation.CAD_export` in `information.json`, and run

```sh
uv run --locked python scripts/sentinel/build_model.py /tmp/Sentinel_Drone.gltf
```

The builder reads the export directly, checks the triangle count against the
record, and writes `assets/*.obj`, the MJCF and `source/cad-manifest.json` (source
hash, microversion, per-mesh hashes and the propeller-to-motor matching) only after
the staged model compiles with the assigned mass and the feet as its lowest point.
All 691,542 exported triangles are kept and grouped only by color and rigid body.
COARSE is Onshape's coarsest tessellation of the exact CAD surfaces; finer
exports exceed 64 MiB and make per-frame camera rendering much slower.

The committed model was exported at microversion `a668233d1f67ae32dea9c710`, before
the legs were moved under the arm roots and the carrier was reshaped to match. Its leg
and carrier meshes and hulls are therefore out of date until it is rebuilt.

- **Frame.** Body FLU (x forward, y left, z up) with its origin at the rotor center
  on the bottom-plate plane (assembly X 98.5, Y −59, Z 0): body = (−X, −Y, Z). The
  feet are 0.1448 m below the origin and the GPS mast top 0.1442 m above it.
- **Mass.** 1.407 kg assigned: the manufacturer's 1347 g with battery plus the 60 g
  CAD mass of the previous carrier. The F450 legs replacing the RB5 gear are not accounted for.
  The center of mass is assumed 30 mm above the plate at the rotor center, and the
  root inertia is a lumped motor/core-box estimate. Onshape has no assembly mass:
  357 parts lack a material, so CAD mass, center of mass and inertia are unknown.
- **Propellers.** Each 1045 propeller is a separate body on a hinge along its
  measured motor axis (6° inward tilt), matched to its motor by geometry and checked
  against the A = CW, B = CCW variant record. Rotors 1 and 3 (rear right, front left)
  spin CW and 2 and 4 CCW, as viewed from above. Thrust and torque coefficients come
  from assumed 1045 coefficients (C_T 0.115, C_P 0.045), giving a modelled hover of
  about 484 rad/s. Spin speed limits use 810 rad/s.
- **Cameras.** `front_down` uses the tracking-camera CAD frame and `rear_left` the
  rear stereo low-Y (left) frame, both rechecked against the camera geometry at
  microversion `a668233d1f67ae32dea9c710`. Reference optics: tracking M0014, 166°
  fisheye; stereo M0015, 85° (the source names no axis; horizontal is assumed).
  Simulation uses pinhole cameras: the fisheye's central focal length (276 px) and
  85° horizontal for the stereo camera (349 px).
- **Collision.** One convex hull per top-level occurrence (33), excluding the
  propellers, which are visual only. Hulls overestimate concave parts such as the
  arms and legs; the feet are exact lowest points.

## Limitations

Fastener lengths, the carrier's physical fit, arm and tray stiffness, payload rating, print results, and leg
material and mass are unverified. Leg positions come from a photo, not measurement. The simulation model's mass
distribution, rotor coefficients, the camera lens models and which camera feeds the
onboard tag detector are assumptions, not measurements. Simulated flight uses an
illustrative controller, not identified Sentinel dynamics.
